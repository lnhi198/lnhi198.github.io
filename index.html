A sugar shack classic, on a record scale

Tire sur la neige is about as Québécois as it gets: maple syrup boiled until it's thick, poured in ribbons over fresh snow, then rolled up on a wooden stick while it's still soft.

Usually it happens at a cabane à sucre with a few dozen people around one table of snow. We want to do it with the whole McGill community, all at once, on campus.

Every person who registers and eats on the countdown counts toward the record.

How the attempt works

  1. Check in

    Show your registration email at the gate and get your wristband.

  2. Get your stick

    Volunteers hand out wooden sticks and guide you to a spot along the snow tables.

  3. Watch the pour

    Hot syrup goes down on the snow in front of you. Don't touch it yet.

  4. Roll on the countdown

    When the clock hits zero, everyone rolls and eats together. That's the record.

Questions

Who can take part?

Anyone who registers: students, staff, alumni, and friends of McGill. Participants under 18 should register with a parent or guardian.

What's in tire sur la neige?

Just pure maple syrup boiled down and cooled on snow. It's sticky and very sweet, so take it easy on dental work.

Do I need to bring anything?

Warm clothes, gloves you don't mind getting sticky, and your registration email. We provide the sticks, snow, and syrup.

What if it doesn't snow?

We'll bring in clean snow for the tables, so the attempt goes ahead either way. Watch your email for any weather updates.

Can I volunteer instead?

Yes. Volunteers help with check-in, handing out sticks, and counting. Email us and we'll add you to the volunteer list.
